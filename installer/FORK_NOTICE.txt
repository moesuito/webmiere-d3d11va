WebMiere D3D11VA Community Fork

This is an independent community build maintained at:
https://github.com/moesuito/webmiere-d3d11va

It is based on KawaiiEngine/WebMiere but is not an official KawaiiEngine release.

Fork features include vendor-neutral D3D11VA video decode, GPU pixel conversion and proxy-resolution scaling, packaged universal audio decoding, and AV1-in-MP4 support with native Premiere fallback for other MP4 codecs.

WebMiere, Mina, Miere, and the original artwork remain the property of KawaiiEngine (Sashimiso). This installer intentionally uses the standard Inno Setup presentation and does not include the proprietary original installer artwork.
